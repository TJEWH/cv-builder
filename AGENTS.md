# Textarea autosave

Every textarea must follow [the shared textarea behavior](docs/textarea-behavior.md). Use `AutosaveTextarea` (or `MarkdownTextarea` for CV content), keep typing in a local draft, and save on blur/Enter. Use `v-model.lazy` when model changes trigger persistence. Publish pending drafts with `commitPendingTextareas()` before snapshotting or replacing an editor, then await its persistence layer's flush before navigating away. Do not save on each input event or after a typing debounce. Shift+Enter inserts a newline; IME confirmation must not trigger a save. Preserve native double-click word selection and press-and-drag text selection; triple-click selects the whole textarea. Textarea commits must not implicitly approve disclosure, upload files, or submit workflows.

# Status notifications and general information

Use the shared toast system (`useToasts`, `useToastWatch`, or `useSaveStateToast` in `src/composables/useToasts.ts`) for user-facing operation updates: saving, saved, loading, copying, synchronization, success, warnings, and failures. Do not add inline status text, banners, live-region paragraphs, or hidden-by-default save messages. Report committed saves once; do not toast on every keystroke or announce an already-saved record on mount. Scope pending notifications and retry actions to the active record/account, retain failed drafts, and keep recovery actions available. Preserve the existing mobile behavior: toasts stay hidden at widths of 760px and below; do not add inline replacements on mobile.

Place general explanations and helper information in the shared `InfoHint` button beside the relevant heading, label, or control. Its tooltip must work on hover, keyboard focus, and touch. Keep essential field labels, record data/statuses, empty states, and explicit confirmation or disclosure choices visible; those are substantive interface content rather than operation notifications. Preserve accessible control labels and `aria-busy` without duplicating toast announcements inline.
